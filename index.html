A STRONG START FOR EVERY CHILD

What early childhood education makes possible

Early childhood education (ECE) supports children’s learning, development, health and well-being during the early years. Welcoming programs give children a safe place to explore, play, communicate and build relationships.

01

Learn through play

Play helps children grow their language, social skills, emotional understanding, creativity, movement and problem-solving.

02

Feel safe and included

Educators create caring spaces where each child’s culture, language, abilities and family background are respected.

03

Grow together

Children do best when families and educators share knowledge and work together around each child’s strengths and needs.

EARLY LEARNING IN CANADA

Access, belonging and quality matter.

The Canada Early Learning and Child Care Act sets out a vision for accessible, affordable, inclusive and high-quality early learning and child care. Programs also reflect provincial and territorial rules, so licensing and educator requirements can vary across Canada.
